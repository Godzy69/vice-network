Privacy Policy

Vice Network and its upload tool, Project Shovel. Last updated 5 November 2026.

Who we are

Vice Network runs the YouTube channels Vice Clips, Vice Radar and Vice Vortex. Contact: setumehta7@gmail.com.

What the tool does

Project Shovel is a private tool used only by the Vice Network team. It uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule videos on channels that we own. It does not let anyone else sign in, and it is not offered to the public.

What data we access and store

  • Our own Google account authorization. When we connect one of our channels, Google gives the tool an OAuth refresh token with the youtube.upload scope. The token is stored on our own server, readable only by our administrator account, and is used only to upload videos to that channel.
  • Upload records. We keep a log of what we uploaded (video file name, title, upload time, video ID and link) to run our posting schedule.
  • We do not collect, store or share any data about viewers or other YouTube users, and we do not use cookies on this website.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data. Videos are sent to YouTube (Google) when we upload them.

Google and YouTube

Our tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the tool's access to a Google account at any time at Google security settings.

Deleting data

Revoking access stops all use of the token; we then delete the stored token and its upload records within 30 days, or sooner on request by email.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new date.
