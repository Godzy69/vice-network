About our upload tool

We run a private tool, Project Shovel, that edits clips and uploads them to our own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the Vice Network team on channels we own. It is not offered to the public.

Clip creators who want a clip credited differently or removed can email us and we act within 48 hours.
